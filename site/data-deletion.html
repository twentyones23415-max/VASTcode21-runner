VASTcode21 Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 14 September 2026

If you want VASTcode21 to stop accessing your Meta or Instagram account, first revoke the VASTcode21 app/integration from your Meta or Instagram connected-app settings.

Request deletion

To request deletion of data associated with your use of VASTcode21, send a message to the official Instagram account @vast.code21 stating that you want your VASTcode21 data deleted. Include only the minimum information needed to identify the connected account. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or security codes.

What happens next

After the request is verified, VASTcode21 will remove data it controls that is no longer required for security, legal, or fraud-prevention purposes. Revoking the Meta/Instagram integration immediately prevents future authorized API access by that connection.

Important

Deleting VASTcode21-controlled data does not automatically delete content or data independently retained by Meta, Instagram, GitHub, or other third-party providers. Their own privacy and deletion policies apply to information they control.
