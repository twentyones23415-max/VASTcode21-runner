Product
04

One evidence loop, three product surfaces.

Build trust by showing how a trading idea was tested, when it should be ignored, and what the trader should learn next.

1. VAST research engine

Original formula R&D; state machine: Wait, Arm Buy/Sell, Buy/Sell, Exit. The aim is disciplined structure, not prediction hype.

2. MT5 systematic validator

Automated validation across symbols, timeframes and market regimes. Exposes robustness, drawdown, failure modes and sensitivity.

3. AI education companion

Explains signals, risk context and validation evidence in plain language; guides the trader toward better process.

From “Should I enter?” to “Is this setup validated, understood and risk-aware?”
Near term: MT5 indicator + research dashboard. Long term: validation database + education + workflow suite.04
User Experience
05

The VAST signal flow is designed to reduce impulsive trading.

The experience separates preparation, action, waiting and exit instead of pushing constant entries.

WAIT

No forced trade. The system stays silent when evidence is insufficient.

ARM

Preparation state: VAST arm buy or sell. User is warned to observe, not chase.

SIGNAL

Buy/Sell state appears only after the trigger condition is satisfied.

EXIT

Exit now state prioritizes closing logic and risk discipline.

Why this matters: retail products often reward action. VASTcode21 is designed around process: no setup, preparation, validated trigger, and exit discipline.
MT5 visuals: rhombus = preparation, arrow = signal, circle = exit.05
