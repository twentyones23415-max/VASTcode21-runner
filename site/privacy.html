VASTcode21 Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 September 2026

VASTcode21 builds educational trading research tools and operates official social-media publishing workflows. This policy explains what information may be processed, why it is used, and how users can request deletion.

Information we process

When a user connects a supported Meta or Instagram account, the integration may receive the minimum account, page, permission, and publishing information required to authenticate the connection, publish authorized content, and read basic publishing results. Credentials and access tokens are kept server-side and are not embedded in the public website.

For VAST Tutor AI / VAST Vision AI beta features, authenticated users may have private educational progress, usage counts, retention preferences, and limited review history associated with their account. Uploaded chart screenshots are processed for the requested analysis and are not intended to be stored as permanent public content.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide requested VASTcode21 functionality, maintain account security, enforce usage limits, operate authorized social publishing, troubleshoot failures, and improve reliability. VASTcode21 does not use connected social accounts to execute trades or manage brokerage accounts.

Sharing and sale of data

VASTcode21 does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by infrastructure or service providers only as necessary to deliver the requested functionality, subject to their own terms and privacy obligations.

Retention and security

We aim to retain only the minimum information needed for the active feature and its security or reliability requirements. Sensitive credentials are kept in server-side secret storage rather than public client code. Users should revoke a Meta/Instagram connection if they no longer want the integration to have access.

Your choices and deletion

You may revoke VASTcode21 access from your Meta or Instagram account settings at any time. You may also request deletion of data associated with the VASTcode21 service by following the instructions on the Data Deletion Instructions page.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the beta evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact VASTcode21 through the official Instagram account @vast.code21.
