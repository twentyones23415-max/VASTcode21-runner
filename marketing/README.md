# VASTcode21 Marketing Autopilot

This folder is the public, non-secret control plane for VASTcode21 marketing automation.

Principles:
- GOLD and BITCOIN only.
- Never promise profits, guaranteed returns, or risk-free results.
- Never publish unverified backtest claims.
- Only promote validated facts from public status files.
- Live trading remains OFF until explicit approval.
- Paid ads remain OFF until explicit approval and available revenue budget.
- Social copy may educate, show research progress, explain product features, and announce verified milestones.

The pipeline produces safe content briefs from `status/summary.json` and `status/autonomy.json`. Publishing/design integrations are handled externally through connected tools such as Metricool, Canva, and Systeme.io.
