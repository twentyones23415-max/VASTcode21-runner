## Why VASTcode21
VASTcode21 is deliberately focused on two markets only: GOLD and BITCOIN. The goal is not to claim that one system trades everything. The goal is to develop, reject, retest, and advance only candidates that survive progressively stricter validation.

## Validation Process
1. Autonomous research generates candidate strategies.
2. MT5 real-tick testing filters candidates using real broker market data.
3. Robustness testing checks multiple periods and parameter perturbations.
4. Shadow-forward observation collects evidence from new market data.
5. Only candidates that clear every gate can become release candidates.
