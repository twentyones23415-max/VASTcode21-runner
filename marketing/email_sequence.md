# VASTcode21 Early Access Email Sequence

## Email 1 — Day 0
**Subject:** Welcome to VASTcode21 Early Access

Thanks for joining VASTcode21 Early Access.

VASTcode21 is a research-driven MT5 development project focused only on GOLD and BITCOIN. The process is designed around rejection and validation—not around publishing impressive-looking results before they are properly tested.

You will receive verified development milestones, explanations of the validation process, and release information when a candidate has earned the right to move forward.

Trading involves risk. Historical or backtested results do not guarantee future performance.

— VASTcode21

## Email 2 — Day 2
**Subject:** How VASTcode21 validates a strategy

A candidate does not become a product because one backtest looks good.

The VASTcode21 pipeline moves candidates through several gates:

1. Autonomous research
2. MT5 real-tick validation
3. Robustness testing across different periods and parameter changes
4. Shadow-forward observation on new data
5. Release-candidate review

If a candidate fails, it stops. If it passes, it earns another test—not a marketing promise.

— VASTcode21

## Email 3 — Day 5
**Subject:** Why only GOLD and BITCOIN?

VASTcode21 deliberately focuses on two markets: GOLD and BITCOIN.

The reason is simple: specialization makes it easier to test deeply, compare market regimes, and build products with a clear purpose. The project is not designed to claim that one strategy can trade every symbol under every condition.

The research engine continues to explore both markets while keeping live trading disabled during development.

— VASTcode21

## Email 4 — Day 9
**Subject:** VASTcode21 development update

VASTcode21 continues to run autonomous research and validation. Development updates are shared only when they are supported by the validation pipeline.

A PASS at any stage means only that a candidate may proceed to the next stage. It does not guarantee future performance and does not mean the strategy is automatically approved for live trading or commercial release.

You will be notified when a meaningful new milestone is reached.

— VASTcode21
