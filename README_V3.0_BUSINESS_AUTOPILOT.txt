VASTcode21 BUSINESS AUTOPILOT v3.0

Purpose
-------
Operate the pre-release VASTcode21 business as automatically as possible at zero added cost while preserving compliance and account safety.

Active automatically while the Windows PC is on
-----------------------------------------------
1. Social publishing
   - uses verified VASTcode21 research milestones
   - maximum one organic Instagram post per day
   - no unsupported profit claims

2. Instagram public comment operations
   - checks connected-account comments every daemon cycle
   - safely auto-replies to simple product, risk, interest, and positive comments
   - high-risk/ambiguous/legal/payment/security comments are escalated instead of auto-answered
   - obvious spam is ignored and logged; automatic hiding is OFF by default
   - reply rate is capped to avoid bot-like behavior

3. Lead triage
   - high-intent public comments are recorded locally under LOCAL_ONLY/business/leads.json
   - no private customer data is committed to the public repository
   - pre-release state never claims that a product is available for sale

4. Growth analytics
   - reads local Instagram analytics snapshots
   - tracks followers and top-performing media locally
   - optimization rule favors real engagement and verified content, never hype

5. Safety
   - Paid Ads OFF
   - Live Trading OFF
   - Sales OFF until a verified release candidate and explicit approval
   - no mass follow/unfollow
   - no unsolicited bulk DMs
   - no force-push/hard reset

Private Instagram DMs
---------------------
Windsor's current Instagram actions cover content and public comments, not private-message actions. Full DM automation therefore remains OFF until the official Meta Instagram Messaging API is connected.

The DM module should only be enabled through an official Meta app with the required Instagram messaging permission. It must answer only conversations initiated by the Instagram user, respect platform messaging windows/permissions and opt-outs, and escalate financial/legal/security/refund complaints instead of improvising.

Zero-cost pre-release operating model
-------------------------------------
Research -> MT5 validation -> robustness -> shadow forward -> verified milestone -> content -> Instagram -> comments/leads -> analytics -> content optimization.

Commercial checkout, licensing, paid ads and live trading remain disabled until separate release gates are satisfied.
